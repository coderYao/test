Ink Koi

笔落成流，鲤随墨行。Where the brush falls, water flows; the koi follows the ink.

  • 拖动画一笔，锦鲤会顺着你的墨迹游动。Drag to paint a stroke. The koi rides it like a current.
  • 新墨夺流：在鱼身上再画一笔，它便改随新墨。Fresh ink takes over. Paint across the koi and it switches to the new stroke.
  • 慢笔浓墨，粗而耐久；疾笔飞白，细而省墨。Slow strokes are thick and last. Fast strokes are thin, dry and cheap.
  • 墨会干，砚台慢慢回墨；墨珠补墨，连珠乘势疾游。Ink runs out and refills slowly. Pearls refill it and surge the koi forward.
  • 浓墨伤鱼：按住 Shift、右键拖动，或点「水」，以清水化开。Thick ink hurts the koi. Hold Shift, right-drag, or tap 水 to wash it away.
  • 流势：一路游、跃、穿圆相，分数最高 ×5；久沉则散。Flow builds as the koi rides, leaps and threads rings, up to ×5 points.
  • 每 300 丈一道龙门：穿过则墨水全满，墨潮退避。Every 300 丈, swim through a Dragon Gate to refill everything.
  • 莲净浓墨；躲开渔翁的钩，别被身后的墨潮追上。A lotus washes ink away. Dodge the hooks, and outrun the ink tide.
