Ink Koi

笔落成流，鲤随墨行。
Where the brush falls, water flows; the koi follows the ink.

  • 拖动 画一笔，锦鲤会顺着你的墨迹游动。Drag to paint a stroke; the koi rides it like a current.
  • 新墨夺流：在鱼身上再画一笔，它便改随新墨。Paint a fresh stroke across the koi and it switches to the new current.
  • 慢笔浓墨，粗而耐久；疾笔飞白，细而省墨。Slow strokes are thick and last; fast strokes are thin, dry, and cheap.
  • 墨会干，砚台慢慢回墨；墨珠补墨，连珠乘势疾游。Ink refills slowly. Pearls top it up and surge the koi forward, harder in a run.
  • 浓墨伤鱼：按住 Shift、右键拖动，或点「水」，以清水化开。Thick ink hurts the koi: hold Shift, right-drag, or tap 水 to wash it away.
  • 躲开渔翁的钩，别被身后的墨潮追上。Dodge the fisherman's hooks, and outrun the ink tide.
  • 莲 出淤泥而不染：净化四周浓墨，墨潮暂退。A lotus heals the koi, washes the ink around it clean, and makes the tide ebb.
